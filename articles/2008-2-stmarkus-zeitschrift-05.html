Ausgabe 2 · Seite 44

44 Koptisch-Orthodoxes Theologie-Kolleg Papst Shenouda III in Germany enters its seventh year In the summer of 2008, the third group of students will complete their four years of studies at the Koptisch-Orthodoxes Theologie-Kolleg Papst Shenouda III in Kröffelbach-Waldsolms. Before them, 29 women and men were successfully graduated from this college since its inauguration in November 2002. They are now holders of the degree of a Bachelor of Theology. Another 40 women and men are currently enrolled at this theological college at St. Anthony’s Monastery, Kröffelbach. The students come from various places in Germany, from France, the Netherlands and Austria, and one student even comes from Sweden. The courses as well as the examinations are held mainly in Arabic, but also partly in German. Recently, the first lectures were translated into English. Main objectives of the Theological College 1. Papst Shenouda III College of Theology was founded to preserve the true belief as it has been passed on to us from Saint Abba Athanasius the Apostolic and Saint Abba Kyrillos I, the Pillar of Christian Belief; it should safeguard that the teachings of the Bible are followed. 2. It should provide an answer to the heresies and other deviations from the right belief in our present time. 3. It should educate teachers of religion and deacons and qualify them for the service in the Coptic Orthodox Church.

45 4. The foundation of theological colleges all over the world is in congruence with the spirit of St. Mark, who established the Theological School of Alexandria as an answer to the non-Christian philosophies there. 5. Close cooperation between this theological college in Germany and other Coptic Orthodox colleges within Egypt and abroad should lead to a fruitful exchange of experiences. Structure of the subjects taught 1) Theology Spiritual Theology Systematic Theology Doctrinal Theology Comparative Theology Comparative Religious Studies Ministry and Pastoral Theology Moral Theology Ritual Theology Ecumenism 2) Holy Scripture Old Testament New Testament Science and Religion Geography of the Bible 3) Science and Religious Art Coptic Art / Archaeology Patrology Church History Hymnology Christian Family Law 4) Science, Philosophy and Education Philosophy and Psychology Sociology Youth Service Christian Education 5) Languages and Literature Coptic Greek Hebrew

46 The teachers are highly qualified clergy, professors and lecturers from Egypt, Germany, France, Switzerland, and Great Britain. Preconditions for enrolment and study regulations at the Pope Shenouda III Coptic Orthodox Theological College 1)The basic preconditions for enrolment are: - being a member of a Christian church - holding a general secondary school certificate qualifying for university study. 2)The application form has to be fully filled out and sent to the college administration at: St. Antonius Kloster D-35647 Waldsolms-Kröffelbach/ Germany. The following documents have to be attached: - copy of birth certificate - copy of passport - copy of general secondary school certificate qualifying for university study - letter of recommendation from respective church or priest. 3)The academic year is divided into two semesters: - winter semester: October to January (14 weeks) - summer semester: February to May (14 weeks) 4)Lectures are held during semester time. One week, i.e. from Friday evening to Sunday, constitutes one block. 5)Candidates must attend at least nine blocks in order to qualify for admittance to examinations. 6)During the holidays between the semesters, visits to the Coptic mother church in Egypt and to other theological colleges are organized in order to give students first-hand information about them.

47 7)Courses completed successfully at other theological colleges of the Coptic Church are recognized as equal to courses done at this college. 8)The study extends over four years, at the end of which a “Baccalaureus in Theological Studies“ (BA in Theology) is granted. 9)Guest study is possible, but does not qualify participants for admittance to examinations.
